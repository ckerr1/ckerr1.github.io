About

I am a doctoral student in economics at Emory University. My interests lie at the intersection of econometrics and statistics.

Before Emory, I read for an M.Sc. in Statistical Science at the University of Oxford. I also hold a B.S. in Statistics and Analytics & Economics, with a minor in Mathematics, from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

Outside of academia, I enjoy rowing and coxing for Emory Crew and watching the Tar Heels play basketball.

Research interests

I am interested in econometric methods, statistical theory, and the role of learning in economic research.
