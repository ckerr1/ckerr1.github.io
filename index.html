About

I am a doctoral student in economics at Emory University. My interests lie at the intersection of econometrics and statistics.

Before Emory, I studied Statistical Science at the University of Oxford. I hold a B.S. in Statistics and Analytics & Economics, with a minor in Mathematics, from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

Research interests

I am interested in econometric methods, statistical theory, and the role of learning in economic research.

  • Econometrics
  • Statistical theory
  • Statistical learning
